The short version

Privacy without surveillance.

Flixate is a personal, local-first watch finder. It has no advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, behavioural profiling, or operator-controlled user database. Your watch history stays in your browser unless you choose to sync it to your own private Google Drive app-data folder.

Effective 5 September 2026

1. Who is responsible

Flixate is a small, non-commercial personal project maintained by the owner of the Flixate source repository. There is no company operating Flixate and no Flixate backend that receives user accounts or watch history. Privacy questions can be sent to the maintainer through the contact details on that repository. Do not put private information in a public GitHub issue.

2. Data Flixate uses

In your browser

Flixate uses browser storage and caches for functionality. This can include:

  • titles you mark as seen, watchlist names and memberships, and removal/deletion markers, together with change timestamps;
  • your filters, saved filter presets, search preferences, and a randomly generated device identifier;
  • cached catalog data, synopses, posters, and app files for offline use; and
  • if Drive sync is connected, your Google account identifier, email address or display name, connection time, and a short-lived OAuth access token.

This information is stored on your device. It is not sent to the Flixate maintainer. The access token normally expires after about one hour and an expired token is removed when Flixate next checks it.

In your Google Drive, if you opt in

Drive sync stores only Flixate state documents: title identifiers, seen or watchlist status, watchlist names and removal/deletion markers, change timestamps, a device identifier, and document metadata. These documents live in Google Drive's hidden app-data folder and are not publicly shared. They remain under your Google account's control.

3. How Google user data is used

Connecting Google Drive is optional and begins only when you choose it. Flixate requests the narrow drive.appdata permission. It uses this permission solely to create, find, read, update, merge, and delete Flixate's own synchronization files in the hidden app-data folder. It cannot browse or access your normal Drive files.

Flixate also reads the connected account's identifier, email address, and display name directly from Google so it can show which account is connected and avoid silently syncing with the wrong account. This information remains in your browser. Google data is not used for advertising, profiling, credit decisions, or any unrelated purpose; it is not sold, shared with data brokers, or transferred to another application.

Flixate's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

4. Services your browser contacts

Using Flixate can cause your browser to communicate directly with:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the app and its catalog files;
  • TMDB, which supplies poster images and entertainment metadata; and
  • Google, whose identity script loads to prepare the optional Drive connection and whose APIs are contacted if you use it.

Those providers may receive ordinary technical request data such as your IP address, browser information, and request time, and handle it under their own privacy terms. Their processing and infrastructure may involve countries outside the European Economic Area. Flixate does not add tracking identifiers to these requests.

5. No tracking, advertising, or automated decisions

Flixate does not use analytics, advertising cookies, tracking pixels, fingerprinting, cross-site tracking, or marketing profiles. Browser storage, the offline service worker, and the Google OAuth flow are used only to make requested features work. Flixate does not make legal or similarly significant automated decisions about you.

6. Legal basis and EU data-protection principles

To the extent that the project maintainer is considered a controller under the GDPR, processing is limited to what is necessary to provide the functionality you request. Optional Google Drive access is initiated with your affirmative authorization and can be disconnected or revoked at any time. Flixate is designed around data minimisation, purpose limitation, transparency, and privacy by default.

7. Retention, control, and deletion

  • Clear this site's browser data to remove Flixate's local data from that browser.
  • Use Export to obtain your watch history in a machine-readable file.
  • Use Disconnect to remove the saved Drive connection from the browser.
  • Use Delete Drive history to delete Flixate sync documents from the connected account. Other connected devices can upload their retained local copy again.
  • You can also revoke Flixate's access in your Google Account permissions.

Local data remains until you clear or replace it. Drive sync data remains until you delete it. Because the maintainer does not receive a copy, the maintainer generally has no remote personal-data record to retrieve, correct, or erase for you.

8. Your EU rights

Where the GDPR applies, you may have rights to information, access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and withdrawal of consent. The local export and deletion controls above let you exercise many of these rights directly. You may contact the maintainer with a privacy question or request, and you may complain to your national data-protection authority. The European Commission explains these rights in more detail.

9. Security and changes

Flixate limits Google access to the smallest practical scope and does not operate a central user database. No software can promise absolute security, so keep control of your device and Google account. This policy may be updated if Flixate's data practices change; the effective date above will be revised for material changes.
